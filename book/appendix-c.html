Reflection Questions for Personal Study

A Guided Companion for Living from the Fourth Dimension

How to Use This Study Guide

This appendix is designed to help you move beyond simply reading this book to personally applying its truths.

Set aside uninterrupted time with your Bible, a journal, and a prayerful heart. Read each question slowly. Resist the urge to answer quickly. Allow the Holy Spirit to search your thinking, and write down what He shows you.

“But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.”

James 1:22

Chapter 1 — The World You Cannot See

What circumstances currently occupy most of my attention?

Am I allowing what I see to become more real than what God has said?

What does this chapter teach me about the difference between faith and denial?

Which biblical example most strengthens my confidence in God's unseen Kingdom?

How can I intentionally develop a greater awareness of God's eternal perspective this week?

Chapter 2 — The Fourth Dimension: God's Kingdom

How would I explain "living from the Fourth Dimension" to another believer?

What does the throne of God teach me about His sovereignty over my present circumstances?

Why does it matter that Jesus Christ is the Creator and Lord of both the visible and invisible realms?

How does knowing that every spiritual being is a creature—and none is God's equal—affect my fears?

In what ways can I reflect the values of God's Kingdom in my daily life?

Chapter 3 — The Rebellion and the Throne

Have I ever assumed that when angels fell, God stripped them of their power?

Where have I been more impressed by displays of power than by the question of who receives the worship?

Does anything in my life — a practice, an object, a source of guidance — send honour anywhere other than to God?

How does knowing that Satan must ask permission change the way I face opposition?

If all power belongs to God and the enemy uses borrowed strength, what should my posture be: fear, fascination, or worship?

Chapter 4 — The Finished Work of Christ

Why was sin humanity's greatest problem?

What did Jesus accomplish on the Cross that I could never accomplish for myself?

Do I sometimes live as though I must earn God's acceptance?

How does my restored relationship with the Father change the way I pray?

What area of my life needs to rest more fully in Christ's finished work?

Chapter 5 — The New Creation

How do I usually describe myself? Does my self-image agree with what Scripture says about me? Which truth about my identity in Christ has impacted me the most? What false labels have I believed that God wants me to reject?

How would my daily decisions change if I truly believed who I am in Christ?

Chapter 6 — The Renewed Mind

Where has my thinking still been shaped more by my past than by God's Word?

Why did God give me a new nature instantly but renew my mind gradually?

Which lie have I believed so long that it now feels like simple realism?

What practically helps my mind agree with what God has said?

What evidence of a renewed mind can I already see in myself?

Chapter 7 — Living by Faith

Where am I waiting for evidence before I will believe God? What has God actually said about the situation I am facing? Do my words agree with His Word, or merely with my circumstances?

What has faith prompted me to do that I have not yet done?

Which of the common misunderstandings about faith have I unknowingly held?

Chapter 8 — Living Confidently in God's Timing

Am I trying to rush God's plan?

Do I approach prayer expecting my Father to hear me, or with quiet uncertainty?

What lessons might God be teaching me during this season of waiting?

How has God used waiting to strengthen my faith in the past?

How can I remain faithful and confident while His promise is not yet visible?

Chapter 9 — Thanksgiving, Peace, and Rest

What am I most grateful for today? What worries am I carrying that I need to surrender to God? Do I truly believe God's peace is available to me? How can thanksgiving become a daily lifestyle instead of an occasional response? What practical step will help me rest more completely in God's care?

Chapter 10 — Living to Please the Father

Whose approval am I most often working for?

What does it mean that the Father is already pleased with me in Christ, and how does that change my motive for obedience?

Where in my ordinary day—my work, my words, my choices—could I begin asking, "Father, how can I please You today?"

What do I do when no one is watching, and what does that reveal?

How have I responded to the Father in my most difficult season?

Chapter 11 — Shining as Sons

Do I think of myself as one who has been sent, or as one who simply attends?

What does it mean to be an ambassador for Christ in my particular circumstances?

Who has God placed near me whose heart He may be preparing? What holds me back from speaking about Jesus—fear, excuse, or hesitation? What legacy am I building, and will it outlast this life?
