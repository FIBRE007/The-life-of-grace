APPENDIX E SMALL GROUP DISCUSSION GUIDE Living from the Fourth Dimension Together Introduction for Group Leaders

Christian growth is strengthened in community. While personal Bible study is essential, God also uses fellowship, accountability, encouragement, and shared wisdom to help believers mature in Christ.

This discussion guide is designed for: • Church small groups • Bible study groups • Discipleship classes • Leadership development groups • Youth and young adult fellowships • Christian families • Personal mentoring relationships Each session includes: • Opening Prayer • Key Scriptures • Discussion Questions • Practical Application • Closing Prayer

Leaders should encourage participation from everyone. The goal is not simply to complete the lesson but to help every participant grow in Christ.

Session 1 — The World You Cannot See

Key Scriptures

  • 2 Corinthians 4:16–18
  • Hebrews 11:1
  • Colossians 1:16

Discussion Questions

  1. What does it mean to walk by faith instead of by sight?
  1. Why is God's unseen Kingdom more permanent than the visible world?
  1. Which biblical example of faith encourages you most?
  1. What circumstances currently challenge your faith?
  1. How can we encourage one another to keep God's perspective?

Session 2 — The Fourth Dimension: God's Kingdom

Key Scriptures

  • Isaiah 55:8–9
  • Romans 12:2
  • Colossians 3:2

Discussion Questions

  1. What does it practically mean to live from God's perspective?
  1. What worldly thought patterns are hardest to overcome?
  1. How does renewing the mind change daily living?
  1. Which habit helps you keep your mind focused on Christ?
  1. How can our group help one another grow?

Session 3 — The Rebellion and the Throne

Key Scriptures

  • Job 1:12
  • Revelation 19:10
  • Matthew 4:9
  • 1 Corinthians 10:20
  • Psalm 62:11

Discussion Questions

  1. Why do many believers assume the fallen angels lost their power?
  1. What does it mean that Satan had to ask permission before touching Job?
  1. How do holy angels respond when offered worship, and why does that matter?
  1. Why does Scripture describe idolatry as sacrificing to demons rather than to nothing?
  1. If occult power is real, how should a believer test what is genuinely of God?
  1. What does it mean that power belongs to God and there is no alternative source?

Session 4 — The Finished Work of Christ

Key Scriptures

  • John 19:30
  • Romans 5:1
  • 2 Corinthians 5:21

Discussion Questions

  1. What did Jesus fully accomplish on the Cross?
  1. What does "It is finished" mean personally?
  1. How does grace differ from striving?
  1. Why do believers still struggle to earn God's acceptance?
  1. How should Christ's finished work change everyday living?

Session 5 — The New Creation

Key Scriptures

  • 2 Corinthians 5:17
  • Ephesians 1:3–14
  • Colossians 3:3

Discussion Questions

  1. Who does God say you are?
  1. Which truth about your identity has impacted you most?
  1. What false identities must be rejected?
  1. How does identity affect behavior?
  1. How can believers remind one another of these truths?

Session 6 — The Renewed Mind

Key Scriptures

  • Romans 12:2
  • Ephesians 4:22–24
  • 2 Corinthians 10:4–5
  • Colossians 3:2
  • Philippians 4:8

Discussion Questions

  1. Why did God give us a new nature instantly but renew the mind gradually?
  1. What does it actually mean to renew the mind?
  1. Which lies have we believed so long that they now feel normal?
  1. What practical means does God use to renew our thinking?
  1. Why is renewing the mind often difficult?
  1. What evidence of a renewed mind have you seen in your own life?

Session 7 — Living by Faith

Key Scriptures

  • Hebrews 11:1
  • Romans 10:17
  • Romans 4:3
  • 2 Corinthians 5:7
  • Hebrews 11:6

Discussion Questions

  1. What is biblical faith, and how does it differ from optimism?
  1. Why does faith always begin with God speaking?
  1. How does faith interpret reality differently from sight?
  1. Why does genuine faith reach the mouth and the hands?
  1. Which misunderstandings about faith are most common today?
  1. Where is God calling you to act on what He has said?

Session 8 — Living Confidently in God’s Timing

Key Scriptures

  • Ecclesiastes 3:1–11
  • Habakkuk 2:3
  • Romans 8:28
  • Numbers 13–14
  • Jeremiah 32:17

Discussion Questions

  1. Why is waiting difficult?
  1. What does God accomplish during waiting seasons?
  1. How can we trust God's timing?
  1. Which biblical example encourages you?
  1. What are you waiting for God to do?
  1. What made Joshua and Caleb different?

Session 9 — Thanksgiving, Peace, and Rest

Key Scriptures

  • Philippians 4:4–9
  • Colossians 3:15
  • 1 Thessalonians 5:16–18

Discussion Questions

  1. Why is thanksgiving powerful?
  1. What usually steals your peace?
  1. How does prayer replace anxiety?
  1. What does resting in Christ look like?
  1. What are you most grateful for?

Session 10 — Living to Please the Father

Key Scriptures

  • Hebrews 11:6
  • Colossians 1:10
  • 2 Corinthians 5:9
  • Ephesians 5:10
  • Galatians 1:10

Discussion Questions

  1. Whose approval do we most often live for, and why?
  1. What is the difference between working for the Father's approval and living from it?
  1. What does it look like to please God in ordinary, unnoticed moments?
  1. How do our words and choices either honour or grieve the Father?
  1. How has God met you in a difficult season?
  1. What one habit would help you live more consciously for Him this week?

Session 11 — Shining as Sons

Key Scriptures

  • 2 Corinthians 5:18–20
  • Matthew 5:14–16
  • Acts 1:8
  • John 20:21
  • Daniel 12:3

Discussion Questions

  1. What does it mean to be sent, rather than simply to attend?
  1. How does being an ambassador change the way we see our workplace and neighbourhood?
  1. What is the ministry of reconciliation, and how do we carry it?
  1. What hinders us from speaking about Jesus, and how might we overcome it?
  1. Who has God placed near you whose heart He may be preparing?
  1. What legacy do you want your life to leave?

Group Covenant

As we continue walking together, we commit to: Seeking Christ above everything else. Building our lives upon God’s Word. Walking by faith instead of by sight. Speaking truth in love. Praying for one another faithfully.

Encouraging one another consistently. Living as ambassadors of God’s Kingdom. Glorifying Jesus Christ in every area of life.

Final Group Prayer

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for bringing us together around Your Word. Thank You for revealing the greatness of Your Kingdom and the beauty of the finished work of Jesus Christ. Help us to encourage one another toward love, faith, holiness, and obedience. May our homes, churches, workplaces, and communities see Christ through the way we live.

Keep our eyes fixed on Jesus. Renew our minds continually. Strengthen our faith daily. Empower us by Your Holy Spirit to live from Heaven’s perspective until the day we stand before You. May every member of this group become a faithful witness whose life reflects the grace, truth, and love of Jesus Christ.

We ask all these things in His precious name. Amen.

Final Encouragement

The conversations may end. The meetings may conclude. This study guide may eventually be closed. But the journey continues. Every sunrise is another opportunity to walk by faith. Every decision is another opportunity to trust God’s wisdom. Every conversation is another opportunity to speak His truth.

Every challenge is another opportunity to demonstrate His peace. Every act of obedience is another opportunity to glorify Christ. May your life continually reflect the reality that, through Jesus Christ, you have been invited to see, think, speak, and live from God’s eternal perspective. Keep walking with Him.

Keep trusting Him. Keep becoming like Him. Keep living from the Fourth Dimension.

"Now to Him who is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us, to Him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus to all generations, forever and ever. Amen."
