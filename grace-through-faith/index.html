Working From Grace, Not for Grace

What happens when grace changes not only what we believe about salvation, but the way we think, work, expect, respond to setbacks, manage resources and relate to people? This book explores the difference between labouring to earn God's favour and labouring from the life, grace and confidence received through faith in Christ.
