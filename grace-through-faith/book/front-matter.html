Dedication

To every believer who has sincerely tried to trust God, yet quietly wondered whether you have prayed enough, believed enough, served enough, given enough, or done enough to deserve His help.

May you discover the freedom of receiving by faith what grace provides—and from that place, find new courage to work, build, serve, persevere, and live.

"But by the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed upon me was not in vain…"

1 Corinthians 15:10

Grace did not make Paul passive. Grace gave his labour a new foundation.
