About the Author

Fadoju Tosin

Practical Christian living rooted in biblical truth

Fadoju Tosin writes about practical Christian living and the relationship between biblical truth and everyday life.

The Psychology of Grace Through Faith: Working From Grace, Not for Grace explores a question at the heart of Christian experience: What happens when grace changes not only what we believe about salvation, but the way we think, work, expect, respond to setbacks, manage resources and relate to people?

Through Scripture and practical reflection, Fadoju Tosin seeks to help readers recognise the difference between labouring to earn God's favour and labouring from the life, grace and confidence received through faith in Christ.

The emphasis is practical: faith should affect how believers approach purpose, responsibility, work, provision, relationships, uncertainty and the ordinary assignments of daily life.

❦

Grace does not remove responsibility. It changes the foundation from which responsibility is carried.

The invitation is therefore not to withdraw from work, but to work differently—to receive, believe, expect, act, learn, persevere and continue from grace.

We labour, not to reach grace, but because grace has reached us.
